After moving our chatbot from a commercial API to a self-hosted open model, our monthly bill dropped by 80 percent. In my view self-hosting is always cheaper than paying per token, no matter the size of your company.

The math is simple: a rented GPU costs a fixed amount per month, and after that every token is free. API providers charge you for every single request, and those charges never stop growing.

People worry about maintenance, but in our experience the model just runs. We set it up over a weekend and have barely touched it since. Any team with one developer can do the same.

If you are a startup, stop paying the API tax. Download an open model, rent a GPU, and keep the savings.
