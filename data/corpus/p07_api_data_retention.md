This guidance summarises how hosted AI model providers typically handle customer data submitted through their business APIs.

Most major providers state that data sent through business APIs is not used to train their models by default. Many retain prompts and outputs for a limited period, commonly up to 30 days, to detect abuse, after which the data is deleted.

Zero-retention options exist for eligible enterprise customers, usually under a signed data processing agreement (DPA). Organisations handling personal data should confirm the retention period, the processing region and sub-processors in writing before sending such data.

Using a hosted API means personal data leaves the organisation's own infrastructure. This is lawful in many cases when appropriate contracts and safeguards are in place, but it requires a documented assessment. Self-hosting keeps data inside the organisation but transfers the full security responsibility to its own team.
