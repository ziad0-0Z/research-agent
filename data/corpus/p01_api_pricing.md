Per-token prices for hosted language model APIs kept falling through 2026. Across the major providers we surveyed, small and mid-sized models are now priced between $0.10 and $0.60 per million input tokens, while frontier models range from $2 to $15 per million input tokens. Output tokens typically cost three to five times more than input tokens.

For a typical early-stage product, usage is modest. In our sample of twelve startups with fewer than ten employees, the median team processed about 40 million tokens per month. At that volume, monthly API spend for small models stayed under $500, and teams using a frontier model for every request spent between $800 and $2,500 per month.

Pricing is pay-as-you-go, so costs scale linearly with traffic and drop to near zero when usage is low. Several founders told us this was the main reason they chose APIs at launch: there is no fixed monthly cost while they are still searching for product-market fit.

The trade-off appears at higher volume. Once a product processes several billion tokens per month, per-token pricing adds up quickly, and some teams start evaluating dedicated or self-hosted deployments. Providers also offer volume discounts and batch pricing, which can reduce costs by 30 to 50 percent for workloads that do not need real-time answers.

Our conclusion: for low and moderate volumes, APIs remain the cheapest option in direct spend. The comparison changes only when volume grows by one or two orders of magnitude.
