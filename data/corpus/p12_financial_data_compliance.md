Regulated financial institutions that use AI models to process customer financial data remain fully responsible for that data, whether the model runs on their own infrastructure or is provided by a third party.

Before using a third-party model service, institutions are expected to complete an outsourcing risk assessment, sign a data processing agreement, confirm the data residency of all processing, and notify the supervisor where the arrangement is material.

Self-hosting does not remove these obligations. Institutions must still demonstrate access controls, audit logging, model change management and incident response for in-house deployments.

Decisions to move customer financial data processing to a new AI deployment model should be approved by the institution's compliance and risk functions before implementation.
