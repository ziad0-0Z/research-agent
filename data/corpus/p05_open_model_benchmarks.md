We evaluated leading open-weight models against hosted frontier APIs on 14 task families relevant to product teams.

On summarisation, classification and retrieval-augmented question answering, the best open-weight models scored within 5 to 10 percent of the strongest hosted models. For many product features in these categories, users in a blind preference study could not reliably tell the outputs apart.

The gap was larger on multi-step reasoning, complex coding and long-context tasks, where open-weight models trailed the top hosted models by 15 to 25 percent. Smaller open models (under 15B parameters) showed the largest drops on these tasks.

We also observed that hosted frontier models improve more frequently; the quality gap on a given task can change within a few months.

For teams whose core feature is summarisation or RAG, open-weight models are a viable choice on quality. For products that depend on complex reasoning or code generation, hosted frontier models currently hold a clear advantage.
