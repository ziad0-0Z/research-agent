# Cloud LLM APIs versus Self-Hosting Open-Source Models for a 5-Person Startup

## Executive summary
A 5-person startup should rely on managed cloud LLM APIs rather than self-hosting open-source models. Self-hosting introduces significant operational overhead and engineering labor, requiring 0.25 to 0.5 of a full-time engineer, which is prohibitive for a small team. Furthermore, cloud APIs provide a lower total cost of ownership at volumes below the 1 to 3 billion token break-even threshold. This recommendation depends on the startup operating below high-volume thresholds where dedicated infrastructure becomes financially justified.

## Findings
1. Renting a single inference-class GPU on-demand costs $1,100 to $2,900 per month for 24/7 operation, making self-hosting more expensive than cloud APIs below a volume threshold of 1 to 3 billion tokens per month. [ev1], [ev2], [ev3] _(evidence: moderate)_
2. Hosted APIs offer lower total cost of ownership for small teams with low request volumes compared to self-hosting. [ev6] _(evidence: strong)_
3. Keeping a self-hosted model reliable in production requires 0.25 to 0.5 of a full-time engineer for ongoing maintenance, patching, and monitoring. [ev7] _(evidence: strong)_
4. Small teams running self-hosted models face operational risks such as out-of-memory crashes under load, driver updates breaking serving containers, and the need for constant monitoring. [ev11], [ev12], [ev13] _(evidence: weak)_
5. Dedicated GPU deployments achieve faster time-to-first-token for short prompts (around 120 milliseconds) compared to shared APIs (250 to 600 milliseconds), though they risk sharp latency increases or errors when capacity limits are exceeded. [ev15], [ev16], [ev17], [ev18] _(evidence: moderate)_
6. Self-hosting requires organizations to manage their own security responsibilities, such as verifying model weights against checksums and implementing access controls and audit logs. [ev8], [ev9], [ev20] _(evidence: strong)_
7. At volumes below the break-even threshold, the GPU sits idle much of the time and self-hosting results in a higher effective cost per token than using an API. [ev3] _(evidence: moderate)_

## Recommendation
Use managed cloud LLM APIs for the startup's current operations to avoid high engineering overhead and infrastructure maintenance. Revisit self-hosting and evaluate dedicated GPU deployment only if monthly token volumes exceed 1 billion or strict regulatory compliance demands in-house data control.

**Impact:** medium

## Limitations
- Cost analyses for self-hosting often exclude the hidden burden of engineering labor and incident response.
- Evidence for operational failures and maintenance burden largely stems from anecdotal forum posts rather than rigorous quantitative studies.
