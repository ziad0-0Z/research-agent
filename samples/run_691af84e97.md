# Research Report: Processing Customer Financial Data with a Self-Hosted LLM

## Executive summary
Deploying a self-hosted LLM for processing customer financial data requires the bank to retain full regulatory responsibility while assuming new operational burdens like hardware maintenance and supply chain security. Open-weight models match hosted alternatives within 5 to 10 percent on standard tasks like summarisation and classification, but incur significant infrastructure and engineering costs. The decision ultimately depends on achieving a high volume threshold of 1 to 3 billion tokens per month to justify the total cost of ownership against commercial APIs.

## Findings
1. Regulated financial institutions processing customer financial data remain fully responsible for that data regardless of whether the model runs on in-house infrastructure or is provided by a third party. [ev1], [ev2] _(evidence: strong)_
2. Institutions deploying in-house AI models must implement and demonstrate rigorous access controls, audit logging, model change management, and incident response. [ev3] _(evidence: strong)_
3. Self-hosting language models introduces distinct security responsibilities, including verifying model weights from public hubs against published checksums and preventing unauthenticated inference endpoints. [ev5], [ev6] _(evidence: strong)_
4. On tasks such as summarisation, classification, and retrieval-augmented question answering, the best open-weight models score within 5 to 10 percent of the strongest hosted models. [ev8] _(evidence: strong)_
5. Open-weight models trail top hosted models by 15 to 25 percent on multi-step reasoning, complex coding, and long-context tasks, with models under 15 billion parameters experiencing the largest drops. [ev10], [ev11] _(evidence: strong)_
6. Maintaining a self-hosted model in production requires between 0.25 and 0.5 of a full-time engineer on an ongoing basis for tasks such as patching drivers, monitoring quality, and upgrading models. [ev15] _(evidence: strong)_
7. The break-even point for self-hosting a mid-sized model compared to API prices falls between 1 and 3 billion tokens per month, as engineering labour costs outweigh compute savings below that volume. [ev16], [ev20] _(evidence: moderate)_

## Recommendation
Proceed with evaluating a self-hosted LLM deployment only if projected customer financial data processing volumes exceed 1 to 3 billion tokens per month; otherwise, rely on commercial APIs. Before implementation, secure formal approval from compliance and risk functions, and ensure dedicated engineering resources are allocated for security patching, checksum verification, and audit logging.

**Impact:** high

## Limitations
- The evidence does not provide specific quantitative benchmarks for proprietary financial text processing accuracy beyond general NLP task categories.
- Cost estimates rely on variable cloud rental rates and generalized engineering labour costs rather than institution-specific internal salary scales.
- The provided evidence does not cover potential impacts on PCI DSS scope resulting from self-hosted LLM deployments.
