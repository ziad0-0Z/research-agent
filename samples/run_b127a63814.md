# Strategic Assessment: Cloud LLM APIs vs. Self-Hosting for a 5-Person Startup

## Executive summary
For a 5-person startup, cloud LLM APIs are the recommended choice due to significantly lower total cost of ownership (TCO) and reduced engineering overhead at low-to-moderate traffic volumes. Self-hosting is only advisable if the startup has extreme token volume requirements, strictly mandated data residency needs, or a requirement for deep model customization. The decision should be revisited if the company scales to billions of tokens per month or if specific regulatory requirements necessitate full control over the infrastructure.

## Findings
1. Cloud LLM APIs offer a lower total cost of ownership for small teams because they avoid the high cost of engineering labor required to maintain self-hosted infrastructure. [ev1], [ev4], [ev5] _(evidence: strong)_
2. Self-hosting requires 0.25 to 0.5 of a full-time engineer's capacity for ongoing tasks such as patching, monitoring, and capacity management. [ev2], [ev14], [ev16] _(evidence: strong)_
3. Most major API providers do not use business API data for model training by default, and enterprise-grade zero-retention options are available for sensitive data. [ev6], [ev7], [ev8] _(evidence: strong)_
4. Self-hosting does not exempt a startup from compliance and security obligations; it merely shifts the responsibility for audit logging, access controls, and incident response from the provider to the internal team. [ev10], [ev13], [ev17] _(evidence: strong)_
5. Dedicated GPU hosting provides more predictable latency and higher throughput stability compared to shared APIs, which may experience variance and rate limits during peak usage. [ev19], [ev20], [ev22] _(evidence: moderate)_

## Where sources disagree
- A conflict exists regarding cost-effectiveness: lower-quality blog sources argue that self-hosting is always cheaper by focusing solely on GPU rental costs, while higher-quality academic and industry reports conclude that APIs are cheaper when accounting for the total cost of ownership, specifically the significant engineering labor required for self-hosting.

## Recommendation
Use cloud LLM APIs immediately to maintain agility and minimize fixed costs while searching for product-market fit. Revisit the decision to self-host only when monthly token consumption approaches the billion-token scale or if specific, non-negotiable data residency or deep customization requirements emerge.

**Impact:** medium

## Limitations
- The research does not provide a specific threshold for when 'low-to-moderate' traffic becomes 'high' beyond the mention of 'several billion tokens per month'.
- Evidence on performance (sq4) relies on lower-quality blog sources, which may not generalize to all cloud providers or model types.
- The analysis assumes a standard startup environment; specific niche requirements (e.g., specialized hardware needs) were not explored.
