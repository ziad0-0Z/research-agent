# Cost Evaluation: Self-Hosting Open-Source LLMs Versus Cloud APIs for Startups

## Executive summary
For startups with low to moderate token volumes, cloud APIs offer a lower total cost of ownership than self-hosting. Self-hosting open-source LLMs becomes more cost-effective only when monthly token volume reaches a break-even threshold between 1 and 3 billion tokens. This financial tipping point depends heavily on factoring in ongoing engineering labor costs for deployment, monitoring, and maintenance.

## Findings
1. Running a single inference-class GPU around the clock on-demand costs roughly $1,100 to $2,900 per month before storage, networking, and monitoring, with hourly rates ranging from $1.50 to $4.00. [ev1], [ev8] _(evidence: moderate)_
2. A single high-memory GPU comfortably serves a 7 to 14B parameter model, whereas 70B-class models require two to four GPUs, multiplying monthly costs. [ev9] _(evidence: moderate)_
3. Frontier model APIs are priced between $2 and $15 per million input tokens, with output tokens typically costing three to five times more than input tokens. [ev10], [ev11] _(evidence: moderate)_
4. Keeping a self-hosted model reliable in production for organizations with fewer than ten engineers requires between 0.25 and 0.5 of a full-time engineer on an ongoing basis. [ev2] _(evidence: strong)_
5. Initial setup of serving, autoscaling, monitoring, and upgrades takes about three weeks of engineering time. [ev7] _(evidence: moderate)_
6. The break-even point for self-hosting a mid-sized model compared to cloud API prices falls between 1 and 3 billion tokens per month. [ev3], [ev4], [ev5], [ev6] _(evidence: strong)_

## Recommendation
Use commercial cloud APIs if your monthly token volume is below 1 billion tokens. Revisit self-hosting open-source LLMs only when your application scales past 1 to 3 billion tokens per month and you have allocated 0.25 to 0.5 of a full-time engineer for ongoing MLOps maintenance.

**Impact:** high

## Limitations
- Evidence relies primarily on general blogs, academic modeling for small engineering teams, and forum anecdotes, lacking direct proprietary startup internal cost audits.
- Specific networking, storage, and precise multi-region latency overheads are not fully quantified in the provided data.
